## Question cards (print, cut, one question per card)

Board members draw three cards each. Use a card at most once per session; adapt the numbers to the pitch.

1. What is the expected incremental revenue of your recommendation in euro per year, and what is the 90 % interval?
2. If the lower bound of your interval is the truth, is the reallocation still worth the disruption?
3. Which single number in your analysis are you least sure of, and how much would it have to change to reverse your recommendation?
4. You cut TV in [country] by more than 30 %. What does the country manager say, and what is your answer?
5. The geo-lift test measured a small marginal return at 2.5 times normal paid-social spend. How did that change your model, and why do you still spend on paid social?
6. Your per-country model and your pooled model disagree for Austria. Which did you use for the allocation and why?
7. How many weeks of data support your Polish estimates? How wide are the intervals, and did you shrink them towards the other markets?
8. Why do channels work differently across our markets? Is it the market or the model?
9. If the 2026 baseline forecast is off by 10 %, does your allocation change?
10. The optimiser moved money to the channel with the highest ROAS. Did it account for saturation, and how do we know?
11. What would you measure in 2027 to find out whether this allocation worked?
12. Which experiment would you run next year, where, for how long, and what would it cost?
13. Your allocation holds the total constant. If the CFO offered 10 % more budget, where would the first euro go, and the last?
14. How much of your reported sales effect is carryover from spend in earlier weeks? What happens in the first quarter after a cut?
15. Which parts of your analysis were written by an AI assistant, and how did you verify them?
16. If the model is wrong about one country, which is it most likely to be, and why?
17. Should we set a floor for the young markets (NL, PL) even if the model says otherwise? What is the strategic argument?
18. Your attribution analysis of the online shop gives paid search a large share of credit. Does that agree with the MMM, and if not, which do we believe?
