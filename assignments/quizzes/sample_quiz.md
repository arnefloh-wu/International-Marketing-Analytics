# Sample quiz: marketing mix modelling basics

Closed-book, 10 minutes. One correct answer per question.

1. What does geometric adstock model?
   a) Diminishing returns to spend within a week
   b) Carryover of advertising effects into later weeks
   c) Seasonality of demand
   d) Competitor reactions
   Answer: b
   Rationale: Adstock spreads the effect of one week's spend over the following weeks with geometric decay.

2. A channel has a ROAS of 2.0 but a marginal ROAS of 0.8 at current spend. What should the budget owner conclude?
   a) Spend more: every euro returns two
   b) The channel is unprofitable and should be cut to zero
   c) The channel is saturated: the next euro returns less than it costs, even though the average euro pays off
   d) ROAS and marginal ROAS cannot differ for the same channel
   Answer: c
   Rationale: Average ROAS summarises past spend; marginal ROAS is the slope of the response curve at today's spend and guides the next euro.

3. Why does media spend that follows seasonality (more in Q4) make MMM harder?
   a) Because Q4 sales are higher and need log transformation
   b) Because spend and seasonal demand are confounded, so the model can attribute seasonal sales to media unless seasonality is controlled for
   c) Because adstock cannot be computed in December
   d) Because the budget total changes across years
   Answer: b
   Rationale: Confounding between budget timing and demand is the classic MMM bias; seasonality and holiday controls are essential.
