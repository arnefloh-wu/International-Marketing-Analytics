# Design patterns for WU decks

These patterns come from the instructor's own hand-edited version of the Business Projects deck (WT 2026/27), where plain bullet slides were turned into cards, icons, charts and steppers. Use them as the default when a slide would otherwise be a text-only bullet list. They follow the template, so they stay on-brand and still look right if the theme changes.

## Canvas and palette

- Slide 16:10, 10 in x 6.25 in. Content area: left margin 0.5 in, width 9.0 in, from about 1.3 in (below the title rule) to about 5.6 in. The footer, page number and logos sit below.
- Use **theme colours, not hex values**, so a restyled template carries through: `accent1` blue #0096D3 (headings bands, thin accent bars, arrows), `tx2` navy #002350 (emphasis: card headings, big figures, the current step, the key callout), `bg2` light blue #E5F5FA (card background), `tx1` black (body text), `bg1` white. For neutral or secondary content use grey: fill #F2F2F2 with #595959 headings.
- Fonts come from the theme: Georgia for titles and headings, Verdana for body. Sizes that worked: 13 pt body inside cards, 16 pt bold card headings, 28 pt bold figures, 16 pt for plain content placeholders.

## Patterns

| Content | Pattern |
|---|---|
| 4 to 6 parallel statements (course synopsis) | Icon rows: one `bg2` rounded-free rectangle per statement, full width, with a line icon at the left and 13 to 16 pt text |
| Objectives or principles that fall into groups | Grouped bands: per group a full-width `bg2` band, a thin `accent1` bar at its left edge, an icon, a bold `tx2` group heading (16 pt) and the group's bullets (13 pt) to the right |
| Two sides of one idea (before/after class, hospitality/retail, AI/self-reflection) | Two cards with a solid `accent1` header band, a small icon in the band's right corner, `bg2` body; between "before" and "in" cards a single chevron arrow |
| Key facts with numbers | Stat rows: thin `accent1` bar, 28 pt bold `tx2` figure, 13 pt explanation to its right; one `bg2` callout box underneath for the takeaway |
| Questions the project answers | Numbered question cards (`accent1` number, text) next to an illustrative map |
| The one central question | A full-width `tx2` navy box with white text, placed at the bottom of the slide so it is the last thing read |
| Countries or regions | A simplified map graphic with bold `tx2` labels and thin leader lines; add a small grey note "Simplified outlines, not to scale" |
| Parts of a sequence (three presentations) | Stepper: chevron/pentagon shapes across the top, current step solid `tx2` with white bold text, the others `bg2`; repeat it, with the highlight moved, on every slide of the sequence |
| Shares of a whole (grade weights) | A native bar chart (not a picture), values labelled, `accent4` bars |
| Schedule of numbered sessions | Card grid, four per row: header strip (`tx2` for graded sessions, grey for coaching), bold topic, arrow `→` task line in `accent1` |
| Facts that belong to two groups (conditions/sources) | Two cards, bullets split over them |
| A person (instructor profile) | Title with the person's photo at the top right, two cards (personal information, contact information) |

## Name shapes and keep them aligned

Give shapes meaningful names (`Stat figure 1`, `Objective band 2`, `Progress step 3`) so the next edit can find them, and use the same x, width and heights for repeated items so the rows line up. Gaps between cards: about 0.15 to 0.2 in. Do not mix two gap sizes on one slide.

## Things to check on these slides

- A title that shares a line with a photo or logo (profile slides) must stay clear of it: shorten the title or shrink the picture, otherwise the last letters disappear behind the image.
- Cards with much empty space at the bottom: reduce the card height, or add the missing content, instead of leaving a tall empty box.
- Icons: use the template's line style in a single colour (`tx2`/`accent1`); avoid mixing icon styles on one slide.
- Pictures that carry meaning (maps, logos, photos) need alt text.
